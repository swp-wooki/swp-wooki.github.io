I am <strong>Seongwook Park</strong>, a student in the integrated BS–MS program at <strong>Hanyang University</strong> and a member of <strong><a href="https://finx.hanyang.ac.kr" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FINX Lab</a></strong>, advised by <a href="https://finx.hanyang.ac.kr/members/director" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Professor Jaewook Song</a>. I also completed the basic and advanced entrepreneurship courses at <a href="https://ipceo.kaist.ac.kr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">KAIST Center for Creative Entrepreneur</a>. I am currently double majoring in industrial engineering and mathematics. My research interests include mathematical optimization, statistical learning theory, and quantum optimization.

Previously, I worked as a software engineer at <a href="https://www.letinar.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LetinAR</a>, where I developed numerical optimization methods for AR optical correction, 3D graphics simulation software, and interactive data visualization tools. I also completed an undergraduate research internship in the Department of Mathematics at Hanyang University under the supervision of <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/minhyunkim/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Professor Minhyun Kim</a>, with <a href="https://jinwookjung12.wordpress.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Professor Jinwook Jung</a> as my co-advisor, studying partial differential equations, functional analysis, Sobolev spaces, and monotone operators.

My current goal is to use machine learning and deep learning techniques to solve mathematical optimization problems, so that machines can make data-driven decisions. This site brings together my projects, technical notes, and course notes.
