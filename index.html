Seongwook Park

BS-MS Integrated Student at FINX Lab, Hanyang University

I am Seongwook Park, a student in the integrated BS–MS program at Hanyang University and a member of FINX Lab, advised by Professor Jaewook Song. I also completed the basic and advanced entrepreneurship courses at KAIST Center for Creative Entrepreneur. I am currently double majoring in industrial engineering and mathematics. My research interests include mathematical optimization, statistical learning theory, and quantum optimization.

Previously, I worked as a software engineer at LetinAR, where I developed numerical optimization methods for AR optical correction, 3D graphics simulation software, and interactive data visualization tools. I also completed an undergraduate research internship in the Department of Mathematics at Hanyang University under the supervision of Professor Minhyun Kim, with Professor Jinwook Jung as my co-advisor, studying partial differential equations, functional analysis, Sobolev spaces, and monotone operators.

My current goal is to use machine learning and deep learning techniques to solve mathematical optimization problems, so that machines can make data-driven decisions. This site brings together my projects, technical notes, and course notes.
